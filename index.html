Surgical tool-tip detector

Oct 2026

XRlabs Real-Time Vision Challenge, 5th place

Finds the tip of every surgical tool in a video frame and names it as bipolar, scissors or suction, at 113 frames per second.

  • Tips are points, not boxes, so the model paints a heat map. One network spots candidate tips, a second checks each one, and confident tips are pinned to the pixel.
  • Trained in one day on six cloud GPUs from 1,741 images, with five videos held back as a fair test.
  • The scoring made one wrong answer cost about five right ones, so I tuned for precision and lifted the score from 0.52 to 0.68.

ConvNeXt-Tiny, heat-map detection, data augmentation

Chess engine

Sep 2026

AI Chessathon, 4th of 500 teams

A chess agent for an Optiver-sponsored competition where every team had to train its own model. Our team reached the semi-finals of the 40-team knockout.

  • A 2,800-line alpha-beta engine compiled with Numba, plus a NumPy evaluation network trained on 4.55 million Stockfish-labelled positions.
  • Every change had to win a sequential statistical test (SPRT) before it shipped. I rejected a re-tune that fitted 9.2% better but lost 117 Elo.
  • Caught early-stopping bias in my own testing: a +167 Elo pass re-ran at +38 ± 44 Elo in a fixed-length match.

Python, Numba, NumPy
